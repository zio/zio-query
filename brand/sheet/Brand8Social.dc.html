Rules
One big mark
The mark fills the right side and runs off the edge. Lines stay quiet; only the strike carries colour.
Text left, never over the mark
Keep copy in the left half, inside the middle 80% of the height, so crops and rounded corners never touch it.
Readable in a feed
Headline at least 56 px at full size; it shows at a quarter of that on phones.
Light and dark
Ship both. Default to dark for Open Graph, where most previews sit on light pages.
