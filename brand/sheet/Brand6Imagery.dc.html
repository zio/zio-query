Visual and imagery style

The mark gives the brand one graphic idea to reuse: lines are requests, the strike is the batch. Diagrams, patterns and icons are all drawn from those two parts, in ink and one cobalt.
6.1
Graphic language
A line is a request
Horizontal, round-ended, ink. Never an arrow.
The strike is the batch
One cobalt diagonal across a group of lines means “sent together”.
The tally is the bullet
Small marks work as list bullets and section markers in docs and slides.
6.2
Pattern
The mark repeated on a square grid, lines faded back and strikes left in colour. Use it behind hero sections, on slide backgrounds and on stickers; keep text off it unless a solid panel sits between.

6.6
Rules
No search symbols
No magnifying glasses or question marks: ZIO Query fetches data, it does not search for it.
No speed clichés
No lightning bolts, rockets or speed lines. Show the fewer lines instead.
Flat colour only
No gradients, glows, glass or 3D. Ink, paper and one cobalt.
No stock photos
Diagrams and real code instead of people at laptops or server rooms.
