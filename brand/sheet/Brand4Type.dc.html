IO Query
The mark
0.82 em wide, as tall as Sora's capitals. It is the Z; there is no Z in the type.
“IO”
Sora Bold 700, −2% tracking, set tight to the mark (0.04 em).
“Query”
Sora Regular 400 after a normal word space. Weight, not colour, separates the two words.
Outlined
Ship the wordmark as outlines, kerning applied, never as live text.

4.5
Rules
Sentence case
Headings and buttons in sentence case. Capitals only in mono labels.
Code is mono
ZQuery, DataSource and every identifier in JetBrains Mono, even mid-sentence.
Lines of 60–75 characters
Body text left-aligned, never justified, never centred beyond two lines.
