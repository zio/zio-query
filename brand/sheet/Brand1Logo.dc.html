Brand sheet · 01 · Logo

Logo

One construction, used three ways: the symbol on its tile, the bare mark inside the name, and the name on its own line for square spaces. The mark is always the Z of ZIO; never set a Z next to it.
1.1
Symbol
Tile. A rounded square in cobalt #2563EB, corner radius 21.7% of its side, the same rounding as the other ZIO tiles.
Mark on the tile. 62% of the tile, centred. The four lines are tint #BFDBFE and the strike is white, so the strike stays the brightest part, just as it is the only cobalt part of the bare mark.
Use it for favicons, app icons, avatars, stickers and anywhere the mark stands alone without the name.
1.2
Construction
120-UNIT GRID · ORANGE DASHES SHOW THE Z
Four lines run from x 18 to x 102 at y 24, 48, 72 and 96: an even 24-unit pitch. Weight 10 units, round ends.
The strike runs from the right end of the first line to the left end of the last, corner to corner, about 40.6° from horizontal. Weight 10 units, the same as the lines, round ends. Only its angle and colour set it apart.
The Z is the first line, the strike and the last line. Their ends meet exactly, so the Z reads as one connected stroke.
The inner two lines complete the tally of four. They always span the full width, crossing the strike.
Colour order never changes: lines in the base colour, strike in the accent. On a cobalt tile the accent becomes white.

1.5
Misuse
DON’T
Stand it upright
Turned a quarter, it is a plain tally and the Z is gone.
DON’T
Colour the lines
Only the strike carries cobalt. All-cobalt loses the batch.
DON’T
Change the strike’s weight
The strike always matches the lines: same weight, round ends.
DON’T
Stretch or squash
Scale it only as a whole; the strike angle must stay.
DON’T
Mirror the strike
Top left to bottom right draws an N on its side, not a Z.
IO Query
DON’T
Change the typeface
The name is always Sora, IO bold and Query regular.
