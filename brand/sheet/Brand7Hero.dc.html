7.4
Rules
One message
Name, one line, one primary action. Everything else lives below the fold.
Text left, mark right
The mark bleeds off the right edge and never sits under copy. Text keeps to its own column.
One solid action
One filled button: cobalt on paper, white on cobalt. The second action is outlined.
